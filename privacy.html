Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

Shopee Review Downloader (“Extension”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy.

No Personal Data Collected

This extension does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All processing happens locally in your browser.

  • No name
  • No email
  • No account info
  • No cookies
  • No tracking
  • No analytics

Local-Only Processing

All reviews and images downloaded using this extension are saved directly to your device. No information is ever sent to external servers.

Permissions Used

The following permissions are required only for extension functionality:

  • downloads — save review files/images
  • activeTab — read visible review elements
  • scripting — inject read-only scripts
  • storage — save user preferences locally
  • host_permissions — required to access Shopee review pages

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use analytics or external APIs.
